## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Welcome to Parking Management System

Welcome to the Parking Management System - your hassle-free solution for finding, booking, and managing parking spots. Whether you're a daily commuter, occasional visitor, or business traveler, our platform makes parking simple and stress-free.

### 1.2 Key Features

- **Smart Search**: Find available parking spots near your location
- **Real-Time Availability**: See live parking availability
- **Instant Booking**: Book spots in seconds
- **Secure Payments**: Multiple payment options with Stripe
- **EV Charging**: Find and book EV charging spots
- **Digital Receipts**: Get instant email confirmations
- **24/7 Support**: Help whenever you need it

### 1.3 System Requirements

#### Desktop
- Windows 10+, macOS 10.15+, or Linux
- Latest Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge browser
- Internet connection
- Screen resolution: 1280x720 or higher

#### Mobile
- iOS 14+ or Android 10+
- Latest Chrome or Safari browser
- Internet connection
- Screen resolution: 375x667 or higher

## 9. FAQ

### 9.1 Account & Registration

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email and follow the instructions sent to your email.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: No, each user is limited to one account. Multiple accounts are against our terms of service.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to Settings > Privacy Settings > Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

### 9.2 Bookings

**Q: How far in advance can I book?**
A: You can book up to 30 days in advance.

**Q: What if I'm late for my booking?**
A: You have a 15-minute grace period. After that, your booking may be cancelled and refunded per our cancellation policy.

**Q: Can I extend my booking?**
A: Yes, you can extend up to 30 minutes before the end time, subject to availability.

**Q: How do I cancel a booking?**
A: Go to "My Bookings", select the booking, and click "Cancel Booking".

### 9.3 Payments

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: We accept Credit/Debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes, we use Stripe for payment processing. Your card details are never stored on our servers.

**Q: How do I get a refund?**
A: Refunds are processed automatically based on our cancellation policy. You'll receive confirmation via email.

### 9.4 Parking

**Q: Can I reserve an EV charging spot?**
A: Yes, select the EV Charging option when booking.

**Q: What if the spot is occupied when I arrive?**
A: Contact our support team immediately. We'll assist you with an alternative spot.

**Q: How do I find my booked spot?**
A: Use the QR code or confirmation code provided in your booking confirmation.

### 9.5 Technical

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: We support the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

**Q: Does the app work offline?**
A: Basic information is cached for offline viewing. However, booking requires an internet connection.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Contact our support team with screenshots and steps to reproduce the issue.

## 10. Troubleshooting

### 10.1 Common Issues

#### Issue: Can't Log In

| Symptom | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Incorrect password | Use "Forgot Password" to reset |
| Account locked | Wait 15 minutes or contact support |
| Browser issues | Clear cache and cookies |
| Two-factor authentication | Enter verification code |

#### Issue: Payment Failed

| Symptom | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Insufficient funds | Check card balance |
| Expired card | Update payment method |
| Security block | Contact your bank |
| Gateway error | Try another payment method |

#### Issue: Can't Find Parking

| Symptom | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| No results | Expand search radius |
| Filters too strict | Remove some filters |
| Wrong date/time | Check selected date/time |
| Location error | Use exact address |

#### Issue: Booking Not Confirmed

| Symptom | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| No email | Check spam folder |
| Payment pending | Wait 5 minutes |
| System error | Contact support |
| Duplicate booking | Check existing bookings |

### 10.2 Error Messages

| Error Code | Message | Solution |
|------------|---------|----------|
| AUTH-001 | Invalid credentials | Check email/password |
| AUTH-002 | Account locked | Contact support |
| PAY-001 | Payment failed | Try another method |

| BK-001 | No availability | Try another time |
| BK-002 | Booking conflict | Check your bookings |
